Half as Fast

What's a Half as Fast you say? Well, simply take your favourite route or trail, find the fastest recorded time and double it. It's a challenge designed for mere mortals, but a formidable challenge nevertheless.

Do you love a challenge, but are too realistic to be motivated by the idea of breaking records? Do you dream about FKTs, but have no natural ability or genetic advantage? Then maybe going Half as Fast is just what you need to have twice the fun.
