The site

This website is a place to record your attempt at doing a route half as fast as the current record.

● You get a green dot if you successfully complete the route within the cutoff time.

● You get a grey dot if you complete the route, but not within the cutoff time.

◕ You get a partial grey dot if you don't complete the route.

Hovering over the green or grey dot shows a few more details.

It's still worth submitting unsuccessful half-as-fast attempts, so long as you had twice the fun of not trying at all!

Guidelines

The ethos of Half as Fast is about be inspired to take on a challenge. As such, we're pretty loose on exactly what does and doesn't count.

Accepted routes: Any route that has inpired you to do attempt a Half as Fast is good enough for this website. It could be a significant Strava segment, a local parkrun, a novelty event like a "beer mile", or an epic multi-day and multi-sport adventure.

Styles: When determining the Half as Fast cutoff time we don't distinguish between unsupported, self-supported, and supported records, or between individual and group based records - we just take whichever is the fastest. After all, you're not here to break records. If you're unsupported and finish at least half as fast as a supported record, then extra kudos to you!

Verification: There is none! When you submit you'll be asked for a link, this can be your Strava recording, a blog post, or an Insta pic. It doesn't have to conclusively prove you've done it, just be fairly convincing to the others browsing the site.
